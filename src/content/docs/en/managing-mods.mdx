The desktop app includes installed-mod management. Review the item and its current state before using the controls shown by your app version.

## Keep the library organized

- Identify a mod by its name or category instead of guessing from a folder name.
- Confirm the selected item before changing its enabled state.
- Before removing or updating a mod, check whether another mod depends on it.
- Keep its source, version, and useful installation notes for later review.

The website demo shows list operations stored in the page’s memory. Its sample state resets when you refresh or leave the page.
